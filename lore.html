Universe & Lore

Story, SS Nightmare & 8 Factions

Explore the cosmic horror world of Chaos Zero Nightmare, the amnesiac Captain aboard the SS Nightmare, and the sociopolitical factions warring against existential collapse.

The SS Nightmare & The Amnesiac Captain

Eons ago, an incomprehensible extraterrestrial phenomenon known as Chaos engulfed Earth, inflicting catastrophic ecological devastation and driving humanity into an interstellar space-faring empire to survive.

You awaken as an elite Imperial Officer holding the rank of Captain aboard the flagship SS Nightmare. During a routine Chaos eradication sortie, an unprecedented spatial convergence wiped your memory clean—a biological impossibility given imperial genetic engineering.

Alongside Vice Captain Renoa and key operatives, you must unravel the conspiracy inside the Imperial Admiralty, navigate ideological warfare between the corporate syndicate Peltion and the religious Holy Crusaders, and discover the true purpose of the Chaos infection.

The Sanity Loop & Deep Trauma

Unlike conventional battlefields, warfare within Chaos Zones degrades the human psyche. Combatants exposed to mental horror accumulate Deep Trauma, requiring psychiatric restoration at the Epione Center in Ark City.

Psychological Awakening: Agents who confront and survive their deepest trauma achieve psychological clarity, unlocking their catastrophic Ego Skills.

Official Narrative Scenario

Main Story Campaign • Part 1: The Descent

Chronicles the voyage of the SS Nightmare through the Sea of Chaos as the amnesiac Captain and crew confront existential corruption and political treachery:

PROLOGUE ARK EXPEDITION

Awakening in the Sea of Chaos

The Captain awakens in the cryogenic bay of the flagship SS Nightmare amidst catastrophic alarms. Vice-Captain Renoa assists in an emergency sortie to repel encroaching Void Aberrations, discovering the Captain's memory has been wiped by high-dimensional interference.

Key Operatives: Captain, Renoa, Luke
CHAPTER 01 SECTOR 1

Fringe of the Ruined World

Deployed to the outskirts of an abandoned orbital colony. The squad encounters contaminated Terrascion peacekeepers driven mad by mental parasites. Nia's swift tactical vanguard secures the communication hub.

Key Operatives: Nia, Luke, Captain
CHAPTER 02 SECTOR 2

Echo of Despair

Investigating a pre-fall cathedral floating in zero gravity. The team confronts psychological illusions manifesting their deepest guilts. Selena's barrier shields the vanguard against resonance screams.

Key Operatives: Selena, Khalipe, Captain
CHAPTER 03 SECTOR 3

Whispering Abyss

Entering deep subterranean caves beneath Asteroid Sigma. Trisa's heavy firepower obliterates nesting Void Swarms, but reveals signs of deliberate biological experimentation by human rogue factions.

Key Operatives: Trisa, Magna, Captain
CHAPTER 04 SECTOR 4

Order in the Shadows

Diplomatic escort mission in Ark City collapses into street combat as Stella Familia crime syndicates assassinate an imperial witness. Hugo and Renoa uncover black market trades of crystallized Chaos cores.

Key Operatives: Hugo, Renoa, Mika
CHAPTER 05 ANOMALY

Trial of the Sixfold Deities

The Captain is pulled into a high-dimensional nexus where cosmic avatars of Vitor, Circen, Diallos, Secred, Nihilum, and Caligo test humanity's resolve, granting the power of Divine Epiphany.

Key Operatives: Captain, Renoa, 6 Cosmic Deities
CHAPTER 06 SECTOR 5

Calamity's Descent

A colossal Calamity Titan breaches the defense perimeter of Ark Prime. The Captain coordinates a multi-vector strike, forcing operatives to face their trauma thresholds to shatter the titan's armor.

Key Operatives: Khalipe, Renoa, Haru
CHAPTER 07 SECTOR 6

The Fractured Sanctuary

The Epione Psychiatric Center is infiltrated. Mental corruption drives patients into violent breakdowns. The Captain personally conducts emergency mental sync to save operatives from permanent despair.

Key Operatives: Captain, Nia, Selena
CHAPTER 08 DEEP CORE

Threads of Fate

Ancient archives recovered from an imperial wreck reveal the shocking origin of the SS Nightmare and the true nature of the Captain's identity as a genetic clone engineered to bind with Chaos.

Key Operatives: Renoa, Captain, Veronica
CHAPTER 09 RIFT

Resonance of the Void

The SS Nightmare plunges directly into the Great Spatial Rift. Navigational systems fail as reality bends. Operatives must rely purely on their psychic Ego links to navigate the impossible tides.

Key Operatives: Luke, Trisa, Hugo
CHAPTER 10 THE GATE

The Gate of Chaos

At the threshold of the primary Chaos Singularity, the Holy Crusaders unveil their ultimate weapon, threatening to cleanse the sector at the cost of billions of innocent human lives.

Key Operatives: Renoa, Khalipe, Captain
CHAPTER 11 FINALE

Nightmare's Dawn

The climactic battle against the Sovereign of Nothingness. Combining the divine blessings of the Sixfold Deities, the Captain shatters the convergence core, securing a fragile dawn for the Ark.

Key Operatives: Full SS Nightmare Operative Unit
Cosmic Pantheon

The Sixfold Cosmic Deities & Divine Domains

High-dimensional cosmic entities observing the Sea of Chaos. When invoked at Cosmic Altars, they grant operatives transformative Divine Epiphanies:

VITOR • The Sunburst Sovereign 33 DIVINE SPARKS

Embodiment of eternal wrath, relentless aggression, and nuclear fervor. Vitor rewards unrelenting offensive tempo, empowering cards with Burn status, critical amplification, and damage scaling per AP spent.

Domains: Aggression • Burn • Critical Burst • AP Escalation
CIRCEN • The Serpent of Decay 33 DIVINE SPARKS

Ruler of biological dissolution, continuous suffering, and toxic osmosis. Circen infuses attacks with compounding Bleed and Poison stacks, dealing massive percentage damage at the start of enemy turns.

Domains: Bleed • Toxic Corrosion • Tenacity Attrition • Debuff Proliferation
DIALLOS • The Iron Arbiter 33 DIVINE SPARKS

Deity of retribution, immutable equilibrium, and retaliatory fury. Diallos strengthens counter-attack protocols, converting enemy attacks into instant retaliatory strikes and unyielding defensive bulwarks.

Domains: Retribution • Counter-Attack • Armor Reinforcement • Stance Mastery
SECRED • The Primordial Cradle 33 DIVINE SPARKS

Goddess of salvation, cellular restoration, and universal purity. Secred bestows permanent barrier synthesis, stress purification, lethal damage denial, and causes all allied strikes to treat enemies as Always Weak.

Domains: Universal Weakness • Emergency Heal • Barrier Ward • Stress Cleanse
NIHILUM • The Void Maw 33 DIVINE SPARKS

Entity of absolute vacuum, dimensional collapse, and anti-matter. Nihilum strips enemy shields, corrupts enemy intent, and turns mental breakdown into terrifying catastrophic spell bursts.

Domains: Void Corruption • Barrier Shatter • AP Steal • Despair Burst
CALIGO • The Shadow Weaver 33 DIVINE SPARKS

Architect of shadows, temporal displacement, and lethal precision. Caligo grants extreme Critical Hit Chance, card cycle acceleration, free card plays upon Critical hits, and phantom evasion.

Domains: Critical Mastery • 0 AP Cycle • Phantom Evasion • Hand Acceleration
