Universe & Lore

Story, The SS Nightmare & 7 Factions

Explore the cosmic horror world of Chaos Zero Nightmare, the amnesiac Captain aboard the SS Nightmare, and the sociopolitical factions warring against existential collapse.

The SS Nightmare & The Amnesiac Captain

The universe of Chaos Zero Nightmare is engulfed by an encroaching eldritch mist known as the existential fog. The remnants of civilization drift inside protected cosmic spheres, reliant on interstellar exploration vessels to scout habitable sectors and harvest energy codices.

You awaken aboard the command bridge of the SS Nightmare—the apex exploration warship of humanity. Stripped of memories, you are addressed only as the "Captain." Through a rare psychic aptitude known as Ego Synchronization, you alone possess the ability to anchor Combatants' minds against the madness-inducing horrors of the void.

As the vessel plumbs deeper into the uncharted Zero Rift, fragments of your past surface alongside dark truths about the six Primordial Deities that engineered the universe's descent into ruin.

Core Themes

Cosmic Horror & Fragile Sanity
Combatants are human minds pushed to their breaking point. Trauma and psychological fracture fuel their supernatural combat potential.
The 6 Primordial Deities
Caligo, Circen, Diallos, Nihilum, Secred, and Vitor represent primordial cosmological laws that govern reality and card rules.
Save Data Metanarrative
Roguelike resets are canonized: each failed expedition is stored as quantum Save Data, allowing subsequent iterations to evolve.

The 7 Major Factions

The 38 Combatants and 49 Partners belong to distinct geopolitical and ideological factions.

Terrascion

EARTH HERITAGE DEFENDERS

Preserves the original cultural records, martial doctrines, and technological roots of old Earth. Disciplined and steadfast.

Notable Members: Renoa, Amir, Nia, Tressa, Selena

Stella

HIGH-ORBIT ADVANCED FEDERATION

Elite space fleet pioneers who command beam weaponry, energy shielding, and advanced targeting arrays.

Notable Members: Hugo, Khalipe, Magna, Mei Lin

Ironrain

MECHANIZED HEAVY ARTILLERY

Industrial legion relying on heavy munitions, mechanical prosthetics, and devastating suppressive firepower.

Notable Members: Luke, Maribell, Beryl, Veronica

Peltion

SHADOW GUILD & COVERT OPS

Covert information brokers and tactical saboteurs adept at stealth strikes, quests, and hand-cycling deceit.

Notable Members: Cassius, Owen, Rei

Crusaders

SACRED ORDER OF CONTAINMENT

Religious knights dedicated to purifying corrupted anomalies and sealing cosmic breaches through unyielding faith.

Notable Members: Orlea, Adelheid

Far East

BLADEMASTERS & PSYCHIC MARTIALISTS

Traditional swordsmiths and psychic dualists who harness the flow of Void, swift multi-hit slashes, and stances.

Notable Members: Chizuru, Rin, Yuki, Fei

Wanderers & Collab Outcasts

INTERDIMENSIONAL DRIFTERS

Enigmatic figures summoned across dimensions or wandering outside the laws of any sovereign faction.

Notable Members: Kayron, Tenebria, Nine
