Game Guides & Squad Synergies

Proven roadmaps from beginner progression to advanced endgame team compositions and Save Data loadout optimization.

🌱

Beginner Starter Roadmap

Starting your journey as Captain on the SS Nightmare:

  • Reroll Targets: Prioritize 5★ Renoa (versatile Vanguard/Damage), Luke (raw Order damage), or Khalipe (indomitable tanking).
  • Aether Stamina: Spend all early stamina clearing the Main Campaign to unlock Basin of Hyperspace farm zones.
  • Account Milestones: Push squad to Lv20 within Day 1 to unlock first Promotion and Epiphany branches.
  • Save Data Habits: Always preserve at least one high-shield deck before tackling Sector 4 elite bosses.
🤝

Top Team Synergies

High-performing 3-member team compositions:

Bleed Chain Combo
Rin + Luke + Nia: Rin applies massive Vulnerable and multi-hit slashes, while Luke converts bleed stacks into lethal Order burst.
Metallization Fortress
Amir + Beryl + Khalipe: Stacks Metallization every turn. Amir's Steel Resolve converts shields into insurmountable team barriers.
Quest Acceleration Engine
Cassius + Hugo + Tressa: Cassius generates high-value Quest conditions that Hugo's continuous skill triggers solve in a single turn.
⚙️

Zero System & Save Optimization

Mastering the endgame roguelike loops:

  • Circen First: Always target Circen shrines early in Chaos runs to obtain AP cost reduction sparks.
  • Trim the Starter Cards: Use Merchant rest stops to purge basic 1 AP strikes and concentrate your deck on 0 AP Epiphanies.
  • Save Value Ceiling: Maximize heat modifiers in Zero System to expand Save Value budget from 200 to 500+.
  • Relic Hoarding: Collect universal rare relics (e.g. Aether Reactor, Chrono Seal) to boost baseline draw.
