👥 Teammate Synergies & The 17-AP Flicker Blade Trick

Rossweisse has unique card interactions that make certain standard supports ineffective, while turning unexpected teammates into game-breaking batteries:

Tiphera: The Multiplier God

Rossweisse’s Sword Tassels carry the Bind tag. When Tiphera equips Snowflake of Our First Meeting, generating Tassels triggers Powdered Snow (+30% Justice DMG, +15% CDMG). Combined with Tiphera’s +200-300% single-hit multiplicative buff, Rossweisse’s nuke reaches obscene heights.

Orlea: The Softy Exhaust Engine

Normal Retain Orlea clogs hand space and prevents polarity cards from splitting. Instead, build Orlea to fuse 3★ Softy and Exhaust it immediately. This triggers Glory’s Reign (+15% ally DMG) and grants a colossal +70% Multiplicative Buff on the finisher.

Fei: The 17-AP Streak Battery

The game hard-caps AP at 9. However, using Fei’s Void Streak passive ("Next X-Cost card triggers as X + 2") allows Flicker Blade Unbound to calculate as if cast with 17 AP! That yields 8 bonus AP tiers = +960% Base Multiplier added before multiplicative calculations.

The CZN Teambuilding Framework

Building endgame squads in Chaos Zero Nightmare is not about putting three random 5★ heroes together. It is an exact science governed by action point curves, hand congestion limits, and multiplicative damage formulas.

1. The Golden Ratio (1-1-1 Rule)

Every top-tier squad adheres strictly to the Golden Ratio:

  • 1x Active Hyper-Carry: Consumes 60-70% of available AP on burst turns (e.g. Rossweisse, Nine, Beryl).
  • 1x Enabler / Sub-DPS: Sets Vulnerability, Flaw Marks, Bind stacks, or provides barrier survival (e.g. Orlea, Selena, Magna).
  • 1x Cycle Battery: Produces bonus AP, accelerates card draw, and filters out dead hands (e.g. Tiphera, Veronica, Mika).

2. AP Curve & The 10-Card Cap

Card economics dictate your combat consistency:

  • Ideal AP Curve: 1.1 to 1.3 AP average cost per card across the 24-card operational deck.
  • The 10-Card Cap Danger: Holding too many Retain cards caps your hand at 10. Cards drawn past 10 are burned, and polarity split cards fail to materialize!
  • Deck Thinning: An 18-card deck with four 0-AP cards cycles 300% faster than a full 24-card deck cluttered with basic attacks.

3. The Damage Formula Demystified

Understanding how buffs stack separates 50,000 damage hits from 2,000,000 damage nukes:

Final Damage = [Base Skill Multiplier] × [1 + Σ Additive ATK%] × [Π (1 + Multiplicative Buff%)] × [1 + Crit DMG%] × [1 + Enemy Vulnerability%]
Additive Buffs (+ATK%, Morale):
All sources (gear 2-pc, potential nodes, flat buffs) are added together. Stacking 50% ATK on top of 200% existing ATK yields only an ~16% actual damage increase (heavy diminishing returns).
Multiplicative Buffs (Final DMG %, Softy, Tiphera):
Each source multiplies against the total! A +70% Softy buff and a +200% Tiphera buff multiply together: 1.7 × 3.0 = 5.1x total multiplier (+410% net increase).

4. Zero System (160pt) vs Chaos Event (130pt) Expeditions

Chaos Event (e.g. Celestial Bulwark - 130pt Limit):
Has a +50% Rare Fate Chance modifier. Use this zone for rapid seasonal farming, acquiring new Epiphany card variants, and testing fresh synergies at reduced enemy stat scaling.
Zero System (Codex 01 Diff 10 - 160pt Limit):
The pinnacle endgame playground. Grants the maximum 160pt Save Data Storage. Route specifically through: Elite Battles > Unidentified Events > Campfire Card Removal > Altar to produce pristine permanent Save Data decks.
