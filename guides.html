The 3-Combatant Rule & Deck Economy

Every team consists of exactly 3 Combatants sharing one combined 24-card deck (4 starting + 4 unique cards per hero) and one shared HP bar. Exploiting enemy elemental weaknesses grants a +25% multiplicative attack bonus and accelerates Tenacity breaks.

Damage Dealer (DPS)
Finisher dealing high single-target or AoE burst (Hugo, Mei Lin, Renoa).
Card Cycler / AP Enabler
Filters hands, generates AP, and prevents bad opening draws (Cassius, Mika, Nia).
Sustain & Vanguard Shield
Absorbs unblocked hits to keep team Stress below 100 (Khalipe, Magna, Hilde).

Proven Meta Compositions

The Apex Hunt Barrage Order / Void / Instinct
Squad: Hugo (5★) + Tressa (4★) + Cassius (4★)

Tressa applies 0 AP Agony debuffs and Shadow Daggers, feeding Hugo’s Commence the Hunt for relentless Extra Attacks (+30% per stack). Cassius manages the draw deck, purges curses, and fixes hands.

Strength: Rapid boss melting without heavy AP expenditure.
Passion Firestorm Passion / Order / Instinct
Squad: Heidemarie (5★) + Hugo (5★) + Cassius (4★)

Heidemarie unleashes explosive Passion burst chains with Linked cards, triggering Hugo’s targeting follow-ups on every volley. Cassius guarantees smooth card draw.

Strength: Extreme AoE wave clear and burst scaling.
The Iron Bulwark Justice / Instinct
Squad: Magna (5★) + Khalipe (5★) + Mika (4★)

Near-zero Stress accumulation. Khalipe stacks Silver Veil defense scaling, Magna casts massive shields and triggers Counterattacks, while Mika accelerates AP through Wave resonance.

Strength: Safest squad for high-heat Zero System codices.

Hero Investment Priority

1. Rank Promotion
Breakthrough levels at 20/40/60/80 using Striker Manuals to unlock higher base stats and card skill upgrades.
2. Potential Skill Tree
Unlock character-specific nodes that permanently mutate starting card effects and enhance critical multipliers.
3. Memory Fragments
Farm set bonuses (4-piece Attack / 2-piece Defense) to reinforce baseline survival before entering Chaos zones.
4. Save Data Carryover
Lock optimal high-roll Epiphany cards and rare relics in the Zero System to create perfect loadouts.
