# Kayron

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Striker  
**Attribute:** Void  
**Faction:** Wanderer  

---

## Role Description

Kayron is an existential Void wielder harnessing the concept of Futility as a combat resource. He creates Futility cards that clog the deck but fuel devastating finisher attacks. Paired with Bria, his enigmatic assistant.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Futility:** Status cards created in the deck that occupy hand slots. Kayron's kit converts them into damage fuel.
- **Black Hole:** Scales with Exhausted Futility cards (+40% per exhausted Futility).
- **Oath of Vanity:** Transforms created Futility into Attack cards with 80% Damage + Healing.
- **Brand of Annihiliation:** Massive 300% AoE damage at 3 AP, self-reduces cost on Exhaust.
- **Death Halo (Ego):** 400% damage to all enemies — one of the strongest Ego Skills in the game.
