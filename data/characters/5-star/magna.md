# Magna

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Vanguard  
**Attribute:** Justice  
**Faction:** Stella Familia  

---

## Role Description

Magna is an imposing Justice-attribute Vanguard specializing in Defense-based damage, Counterattack procs, and Crystallization shield mechanics. She combines tank durability with surprising offensive output.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Counterattack:** Triggers defensive damage procs when shields are gained or when enemies attack.
- **Crystallization:** Stacking mechanic enhancing shield efficiency and enabling Counterattack triggers.
- **Defense Scaling:** All Attack cards deal Defense-based Damage.
