# Heidemarie

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Ranger  
**Attribute:** Passion  
**Faction:** Limited (Terrascion)  

---

## Role Description

Heidemarie is a high-impact ranged Ranger specializing in sustained burst damage and aggressive card manipulation. As a Limited unit, she brings elite combat capability built around card **synergy chains** and **Linked** mechanics, making her a cornerstone in Passion-based damage comps.

## Key Mechanics

- **Linked:** Heidemarie's signature mechanic. Cards gain **Link status** enabling synergy plays and follow-up bonuses when linked cards are played in sequence.
- **Effulgent Blade Chain:** Her starting card tree emphasizes consecutive strikes and temporary damage amplification.
- **Passion Attribute:** Excels at raw single-target and AoE damage output.
