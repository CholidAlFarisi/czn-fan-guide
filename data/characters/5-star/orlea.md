# Orlea

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Psionic  
**Attribute:** Instinct  
**Faction:** The Holy Crusaders  

---

## Role Description

Orlea is a devout priestess of the Holy Crusaders, wielding divine summoning powers to create miniature combat familiars (Softie, Swiftie, Toughie Creations) that scale through Retain mechanics. She provides healing, team buffs, and progressive damage growth.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Creation Cards (★/★★/★★★):** Summoned familiars in three tiers:
  - **Softie ★/★★/★★★:** Skill cards granting team damage buffs that scale with Retain.
  - **Swiftie ★/★★/★★★:** Attack cards dealing scaling damage boosted by Retain.
  - **Toughie ★/★★/★★★:** Shield/Heal cards scaling with Retain.
- **Retain Economy:** Orlea's core power curve is driven by keeping Creation cards in hand to trigger their Retain bonuses repeatedly.
- **Will of Light:** Activates ALL Retain effects in hand simultaneously — the keystone burst enabler.
