# Rin

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Striker  
**Attribute:** Void  
**Faction:** Wanderer  

---

## Role Description

Rin is a solitary wandering swordswoman seeking love across the stars. In combat she channels devastating Void sword arts through alternating stances — Dark Cloud Stance and Dark Mist Blade Art — to deliver overwhelming single-target damage.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Dark Cloud Stance / Dark Mist Blade Art:** Alternating sword forms. Entering Dark Cloud Stance via Drawing Slash enables Dark Mist Blade Art bonuses on subsequent attacks.
- **Haste Synergy:** Drawing Slash and Annihilation feature Haste, allowing multi-hit combos without advancing enemy turns.
- **Stance Retention:** Dark Mist Inner Art enables retaining stances across turns for extended burst windows.

---

## Ego Skill

Dark Mist Finale — Consumes all stance states to unleash a catastrophic single-target Void strike dealing massive damage proportional to stance stacks.
