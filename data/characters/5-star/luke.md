# Luke

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Hunter  
**Attribute:** Order  
**Faction:** Ironrain (Wolf Platoon)  

---

## Role Description

Commander of the elite Wolf Platoon, Luke is a precision Hunter wielding dual handguns and specialized ammunition. He excels at tracking enemies with Mark debuffs and deploying Bullet cards for devastating multi-hit burst combos.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Mark:** Tracking debuff placed on enemies concentrating bullet impacts and amplifying critical vulnerability.
- **Bullet Cards:** Magnum Shot (0 AP Ephemeral), Silver Shot (0 AP), Uranium Shot (0 AP), Tracer (0 AP with Mark 1). All Exhaust after use.
- **Finisher Round:** Ultimate Bullet card unlocked after 5 Handgun Bullet plays.
