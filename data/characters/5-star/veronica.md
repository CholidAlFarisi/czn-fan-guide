# Veronica

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Ranger  
**Attribute:** Passion  
**Faction:** Stella Familia  

---

## Role Description

Veronica is a Passion-attribute Ranger from Stella Familia who deploys autonomous Ballista artillery systems. Her unique Reload/Ballista mechanics generate Ephemeral Ballista cards that deal automatic Extra Attack damage at end of turn.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Ballista System:** Creates Ephemeral Ballista cards (Micro/Standard/Enhanced/Giant/Piercing/Shelling) that fire automated Extra Attacks at end of turn.
- **Reload:** Resource mechanic fueling Ballista deployment and enhancing their damage output.
- **No AP Cost (Ø):** Ballista cards cost Ø (no AP), functioning as free end-of-turn automated damage.
- **Team Synergy:** Pendant of Resolution triggers Reload when other combatants use Skill cards.
