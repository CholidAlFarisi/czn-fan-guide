## Overview
Rossweisse is a premier 5★ seasonal Striker introduced in Galactic Disaster Season 5 "A Promise Upon the Snow" (1st Anniversary, October 21, 2026). As the Apostle of Kyrken, Goddess of Cycles, her visual motif incorporates the traditional five colors (Obangsaek) and the legendary Saingeom blade to ward off calamity. Her core combat mechanic revolves around the polarizing signature card "Flicker Blade", which divides into "Shard: Light" and "Shard: Dark" at opposite ends of the hand, triggering synergistic effects, generating Sword Tassels, and merging back into the devastating Equilux burst attack.

- **Breakdown Card**: `Despair` (0 AP, Atk) — *Has a low chance for unexpected results to occur on use.*
