# Renoa

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Hunter  
**Attribute:** Void  
**Faction:** Terrascion  

---

## Role Description

Renoa is a Void-attribute Hunter specializing in Dirge Bullet generation and discard synergies. She creates and manipulates Bullet cards within the deck, converting discards into Extra Attack damage.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Dirge Bullet:** Signature card that deals 160% damage on play and triggers 80% Extra Attack when discarded to the Graveyard.
- **Discard Synergy:** Flower of Devoured Fate and Resolute Assault reward discarding, fueling Dirge Bullet creation loops.
- **Scaling Burst:** Ballad of Pitch Black gains +20% per Dirge Bullet in hand; Resolute Assault gains +50% per Dirge Bullet discarded.
