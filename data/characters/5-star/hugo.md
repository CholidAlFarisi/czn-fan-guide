# Hugo

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Hunter  
**Attribute:** Order  
**Faction:** Stella Familia  

---

## Role Description

Hugo is a predatory Order-aligned Hunter capable of unleashing rapid, relentless Extra Attacks. By stacking his signature **Commence the Hunt** mechanic, Hugo converts every targeted attack into a devastating barrage of follow-up strikes.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Commence the Hunt:** Whenever Hugo uses a Targeting Attack Card, an **Extra Attack** is triggered. Each stack grants **+30% Extra Attack Damage** (stackable up to 5 times).
- **Extra Attack Multipliers:** His follow-ups scale aggressively with team Critical Chance and Bonus Damage stats.
- **Haste Integration:** Cards like *Fan of Daggers* and *Quick Fix* possess the **Haste** tag, dealing multi-hit damage without advancing the enemy's Action Count.

---

## Notable Epiphany Cards

- **Tier III/IV Epiphany:** Upgrades *Hunting Instincts* to grant immediate Action Point refunds upon reaching max Commence the Hunt stacks.
- **Divine Epiphany:** Enhances *Fixer* to cause all Extra Attacks to bypass enemy Tenacity shields entirely.
