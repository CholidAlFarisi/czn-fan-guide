# Mei Lin

**Rarity:** 5★  
**Class:** Striker  
**Attribute:** Passion  
**Faction:** Terrascion (Planet-Born)  

---

## Role Description

Mei Lin is a fierce Passion-attribute Striker from the planet-born lineage of Terrascion. She excels at aggressive melee combos with the Ember debuff and martial arts chains, delivering explosive fire-type burst damage.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Ember:** Elemental debuff placed on enemies. Stacks fire resonance that detonates for massive Passion damage.
- **Combo Chains:** Rising Dragon Spire adds extra hits on combos, enabling massive burst turns.
- **Spirit of the Aromata:** Retained buff card providing +20% Damage per card used across the turn.
- **Flame Dragon:** Dual-purpose card (both an Upgrade and Attack form) central to her kit.

## Ego Skill

Dragon's Wrath — Channels all accumulated Ember stacks into a catastrophic Passion explosion dealing massive AoE fire damage.
