# Haru

**Rarity:** 4★  
**Class:** Striker  
**Attribute:** Justice  
**Faction:** Terrascion  

---

## Role Description

Haru is a Justice-attribute Striker specializing in Anchor-based burst damage and Justice scaling. She applies combo chains via Anchor Shot and benefits from Justice-scaling cards like Power Anchor and Power Charge.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Anchor Shot:** Primary damage dealer — stacks +60% damage per use (max 5 stacks).
- **Lift Anchor:** 0-cost prep enabling Anchor Shot spam (+1 Cost, +70% Damage).
- **Justice Scaling:** Power Anchor and Power Charge provide Justice-based burst (single-target +80% bonus).
- **Charge Energy:** Retain buff granting +30% Attack card damage for 1 turn.
- **Shield Generation:** Anchor Drop and Lift Anchor enable sustained shielding.
