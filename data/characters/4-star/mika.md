# Mika

**Rarity:** 4★  
**Class:** Controller  
**Attribute:** Justice  
**Faction:** Terrascion [SS Edenity]  

---

## Role Description

Mika is a Justice-attribute Controller specializing in Wave generation, Hydrodynamic Resonance, and water-based healing/shielding. She excels at Action Point generation through Wave stacks and provides potent team sustain.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Wave Core:** Mika's defining resource. Generated by Deluge (start-of-turn gain), Tactical Analysis, and Blessing of Waves (+30% healing per AP).
- **Hydrodynamic Resonance:** At 3 Wave stacks, triggers to generate additional AP or amplify healing.
- **Blessing of Waves:** Retain skill providing scaling healing based on current AP.
- **Source of Water:** 0-cost heal +1 AP enables Wave loop starters.
- **Whirlpool:** AoE damage + healing based on enemies hit.
- **Tactical Maneuver:** Large team shield (+2 Tenacity Damage reflect).
