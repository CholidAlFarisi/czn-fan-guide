# Tressa

**Rarity:** 4★  
**Class:** Striker  
**Attribute:** Void  
**Faction:** Terrascion  

---

## Role Description

Tressa is a Void-attribute Striker specializing in Agony damage-over-time and burst weapon combos. She applies stacking Agony debuffs and converts them into massive burst damage via Vital Attack and Advanced Shadow Dagger chains.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Agony Application:** Applies stacking DoT via Curse (1 Agony per Attack), Cursed Gouge (2 Agony), and her dagger cards.
- **Burst Conversion:** Vital Attack gains +50% damage per Agony stack on target.
- **Shadow Dagger Loop:** 0-cost Agony applier that Exhausts; Advanced Shadow Dagger retains and retrieves from graveyard.
- **Touch of Darkness:** Reliable heal enabling extended Agony application turns.
- **Resolute Blitz:** Defense-based Damage + Counterattack with Pulverize tag.
