# Owen

**Rarity:** 4★  
**Class:** Striker

## Role Description

Owen is a Passion-attribute Striker specializing in Break Armor vuln applications and Gale Strike burst combos. He excels at applying Vulnerable debuffs and following up with high-damage strikes.

- **Break Armor:** Applies Vulnerable 2 debuff enabling +25% weakness damage.
- **Gale Strike:** 250% damage + 80% Shield — primary burst tool.
- **Wind Charge:** Grants +1 hit to next Attack card (combo extender).
- **Wind Riding:** 150% Shield + Wind Charge recycle from discard.
- **Wind Slash:** 220% AoE damage to all enemies.
- **Shield Access:** Weapon Barrier (likely Weapon Block) provides 100% Shield.
