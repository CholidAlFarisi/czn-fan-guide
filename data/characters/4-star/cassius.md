## Role Description

Cassius is an Instinct-attribute Controller from Peltion specializing in Quest mechanics, dice rolling, and card manipulation. He excels at probabilistic engines and burst combo turns via Devil Dice and Quest synergies.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Quest Engine:** Pop Eyed Popper starts random Quests; each Quest completion reduces Dice Trick cost.
- **Dice Trick:** Primary damage dealer — scales down in cost as Quests advance.
- **Straight/Fouse/Triple Lines:** Quest rewards creating utility cards:
  - **Straight:** Grants 2 Morale, Resolve, Fortitude for 1 turn.
  - **Triple 0:** Scales with Attack card usage count.
  - **Triple 1:** 90% Damage + 90% Heal.
- **Mana Field + Joker:** Reliable heal and card draw.
- **Shuffle:** Full hand recycle for card access.
