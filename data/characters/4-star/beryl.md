# Beryl

**Rarity:** 4★  
**Class:** Vanguard  
**Attribute:** Justice

## Role Description

Beryl is a Justice-attribute Vanguard specializing in team shielding and burst damage via Jack-of-all-trades utility. She excels at consistent team protection and enabling ally burst turns.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Shield Core:** Beryl provides consistent team shielding via Barrier, Shelter Defense, and Shelter Hold (220%).
- **Charged Shot:** Retain skill dealing 200% damage with +80% damage when retained.
- **Guilty Pleasure:** 0-cost draw 3 for card cycling.
- **Heavy Weapons Specialist:** Creates Opening Found or Charged Shot with Exhaust and -1 cost.
- **Shelter Strike:** Deals damage equal to current Shield owned (synergy with shielding).
- **Unlimited Firepower:** Grants shield +80% damage boost to next Attack card.
