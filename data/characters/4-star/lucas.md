# Lucas

**Rarity:** 4★  
**Class:** Hunter  
**Attribute:** Passion

## Role Description

Lucas is a Passion-attribute Hunter specializing in Bullet card mechanics and Bombardment combos. He excels at creating and chaining Ballista and Bullet cards for automated Extra Attack damage.

## Key Unique Mechanics

- **Bullet Core:** Lucas creates and fires Bullet cards (Magnum Shot, Machine Gun, Silver Shot, Uranium Shot, S.S.S) that exhaust after use.
- **Ballista Synergy:** Extended Magazine creates Launcher Bullets; Shielding Incendiary Bomb provides shielding.
- **R.P.G-7:** Deals 40 Fixed Damage when Bullet cards exhaust.
- **Bullet Damage Scaling:** S.S.S grants +40% Bullet card damage; Silver Shot doubles damage on Weakness.
- **Haste Bullets:** Cold Shot (+1 Action Count to enemies) enables Bullet spam turns.
