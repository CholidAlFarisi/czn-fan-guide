# Team Building Guide

Team building in *Chaos Zero Nightmare* revolves around creating synergistic squads of **three Combatants** sharing one HP pool and one combined deck. A well-built team balances damage, defense, card cycling, and attribute coverage.

---

## 1. Core Principles

### The 3-Combatant Rule
- Every battle team consists of exactly **3 Combatants**.
- They share **one unified HP bar**.
- Each character adds **8 cards** (4 starting + 4 unique) to form the starting **24-card deck**.

### Attribute Matching
- Matches should exploit the **recommended attribute** of each encounter.
- Hitting an enemy's attribute weakness provides **+25% attack damage** and significantly increases break damage.
- Since characters have no weaknesses to each other, prioritize matching enemy weaknesses.

### Deck AP Economy
- You have **3 AP per turn** (unless modified by cards/relics).
- Balance card costs: a healthy deck includes 0-cost enablers, 1-cost core skills, and 2+ cost finishers.
- Avoid deck bloat with too many 2-cost or 3-cost cards without AP-generating support.

## 2. Meta Synergies & Engine Archetypes

### Extra Attack & Targeting Engine
- **Core Unit:** Hugo (Commence the Hunt).
- **Synergy:** Triggers Extra Attacks on targeted attack plays (+30% per stack).
- **Best Partners:** Multi-hit attackers, card draw engines, and Haste card users.

### Discard & Agony Engine
- **Core Units:** Tressa + Nia / Renoa.
- **Synergy:** Tressa applies Agony while Renoa and Nia leverage discard mechanics (Flower of Devoured Fate, Mute Accent, Adagio).
- **Payoff:** Constant hand recycling, extra attacks on discard, and massive Vital Attack / Black Hole finishers.

### Wave & AP Acceleration Engine
- **Core Unit:** Mika (Wave mechanic).
- **Synergy:** Accumulating 3 Wave stacks generates bonus AP, allowing heavy spenders to cast expensive high-damage cards every turn.
- **Best Partners:** Haru (Anchor Shot), Beryl (heavy launchers), or Mei Lin.

### Linked & Burst Chain Engine
- **Core Unit:** Heidemarie (Linked cards).
- **Synergy:** Consecutive linked plays grant multiplicative damage buffs and Retain continuity.
- **Best Partners:** Passion Strikers (Mei Lin, Owen) and fast cyclers (Cassius).

## 3. Notable Team Compositions

### Composition A: The Apex Hunt Barrage (Order / Void / Instinct)
- **Combatants:** Hugo (5★ Order Hunter) + Tressa (4★ Void Striker) + Cassius (4★ Instinct Controller)
- **Role Distribution:**
  - **Hugo:** Primary DPS via Commence the Hunt extra attacks.
  - **Tressa:** Agony DoT debuffs and zero-cost Shadow Dagger spam to feed Hugo's hunt triggers.
  - **Cassius:** Card cycling, Quest engine, Joker/Shuffle hand management, and emergency healing.
- **Why it Works:** Tressa's low-cost cards rapidly trigger Hugo's hunting instincts without draining AP, while Cassius fixes hands and accelerates combo turns.

### Composition B: Passion Firestorm (Passion / Order / Instinct)
- **Combatants:** Heidemarie (5★ Passion Ranger) + Hugo (5★ Order Hunter) + Cassius (4★ Instinct Controller)
- **Role Distribution:**
  - **Heidemarie:** High-burst Passion DPS with Linked chains.
  - **Hugo:** Extra Attack follow-ups on every targeting attack.
  - **Cassius:** Hand filtering, quest buffs, and AP-efficient utility.
- **Why it Works:** Blends high raw damage with rapid card turnover and relentless follow-up hits.

### Composition C: The Iron Bulwark (Justice / Instinct)
- **Combatants:** Magna (5★ Justice Vanguard) + Khalipe (5★ Instinct Vanguard) + Mika (4★ Justice Controller)
- **Role Distribution:**
  - **Khalipe:** Defense-based damage scaling and Silver Veil.
  - **Magna:** Heavy team shields and Counterattack triggers.
  - **Mika:** Wave sustain, HP recovery, and AP acceleration.
- **Why it Works:** Near-impenetrable defense preventing all Stress accumulation, with retaliatory Counterattacks and defense-scaling nukes grinding down bosses.

---

## 4. Team Building Checklist

Before heading into difficult Chaos sectors or Codices, verify:
- [ ] Do you have at least one character matching the recommended enemy attribute?
- [ ] Is there adequate defense (shields/healing) to prevent Stress accumulation?
- [ ] Does the deck have sufficient 0-cost or 1-cost cards to maintain turn flow?
- [ ] Is there a card cycling / draw mechanism (Nia, Cassius, or neutral draw cards)?
- [ ] Do card effects synergize (e.g., discard triggers, extra attack procs, morale stacking)?
