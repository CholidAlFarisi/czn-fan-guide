# Beginner's Guide — Getting Started in CZN

This guide covers everything a new Captain needs to know for their first days in *Chaos Zero Nightmare*, from onboarding to daily routines and early progression milestones.

---

## 1. First Things to Do

| Step | What to Do |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Finish the tutorial (Prologue) — opens the shop, mailbox, and Rescue banner. |
| **2** | Claim your **entire mailbox** — launch gifts, event rewards, and pre-registration bundles all convert to pull currency. |
| **3** | Reach **Chapter 1 Episode 4** to unlock the *Register Coupon* feature, then redeem any active redemption codes. |
| **4** | Push the main story — it hands out the most resources per minute in the early game. |

## 2. Understanding the HUD (Combat Screen)

### Character Side (Left)
| # | Element | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Team HP | Shared health bar for all 3 combatants |
| 2 | Stress Meter | Purple gauge tracking mental strain; full = Mental Breakdown |
| 3 | Character Resource | Unique per-character mechanic (e.g., Wave, Mark, Commence the Hunt) |
| 4 | Buffs & Debuffs | Active effects with stack counts |
| 5 | Draw Pile | Remaining cards in draw pile |
| 6 | Ego Points (EP) | Energy for ultimate Ego Skill |
| 7 | Ego Skills | Ultimate skill requiring EP; usable once per battle (twice if recovering from Breakdown) |
| 8 | Card Owner | Identifies which character owns each card |
| 9 | Card Cost | AP required to play (0, 1, 2, 3, or X) |
| 10 | Card Title & Type | Name + type (Attack / Skill / Upgrade / Status Ailment) |
| 11 | Card Attribute | Element (Passion, Void, Instinct, Order, Justice) |
| 12 | Action Points | Points consumed to play cards (3 per turn) |

### Enemy Side (Right)
| # | Element | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 13 | Combat Rounds | Number of rounds in the encounter |
| 14 | Action Count | Decreases with each card played; at 0 enemy attacks. ∞ = attacks at end of your turn |
| 15 | Attribute Weakness | Enemy's elemental weakness |
| 16 | Anticipated Action | The attack or skill the enemy will perform |
| 17 | Menu | Settings and exit |
| 18 | Auto Battle | System plays for you (unavailable in Chaos Manifestations) |
| 19 | Total Damage | Damage dealt by your combo |
| 20 | Enemy HP Bar | Total enemy health |
| 21 | Tenacity Gauge | Break meter; depleting triggers Ravage State |
| 22 | Enemy Buffs & Debuffs | Active enemy effects |
| 23 | Graveyard Pile | Cards discarded after use; reshuffles when draw pile empties |
| 24 | Protos Skill | Sandglass icon — undo last action (uses Sands of Time, max 15 per match) |
| 25 | End Turn | Blue play icon — discard remaining cards and AP |

## 3. Aether Management

**Aether** is your stamina resource — it regenerates over time and should never sit at the cap.

| Phase | Where to Spend Aether |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Early Game** | Main story stages (highest resource-per-minute yield) |
| **Level 25+** | Simulation: Memory Fragments (60 Aether per run) — primary gear source |
| **Daily** | Repeatable stages for level-up and upgrade materials |

> **Tip:** Unspent Aether is wasted. Always spend before the daily reset.

---

## 4. Reroll Considerations

Before spending all pull currency, decide whether to reroll for a strong opener:
- **Brand-new account:** Consider rerolling for a flexible 5★ (consult the current tier list).
- **Happy with your start:** Save currency for a banner you actually want.
- **Pity system** makes pulls predictable — budget with the Pity & Pull Planner.

## 7. Cards 101

- Each character has **4 starting cards** plus **4 unique cards**.
- **Balance AP cost** when selecting cards — avoid all high-cost or all low-cost builds.
- **Draw 5 cards per turn** and spend 3 AP.
- Cards have **synergies** triggered by correct order and card types.
- **Epiphany** (yellow glint + light bulb icon) unlocks stronger cards at 0 AP cost.
- **Ego Skills** require EP accumulation and are usable once per combat (twice if recovering from breakdown).

---

## 8. Stress & Survival

- **Stress** builds from unblocked damage and prolonged fights.
- At **100 Stress**, a character enters **Mental Breakdown**:
  - Team HP reduced by 1/3.
  - Character immobilized.
  - All their cards become Breakdown Cards.
  - Play 5 Breakdown Cards to recover.
- **Prevent stress** with shields, healing, and breaking enemy Tenacity bars.

---

## 9. Ark City Quick Start

Unlocked at **Story Chapter 2 #01-2**:
- **Policy Office:** Assign missions, generate resources, increase happiness.
- **Garden Café:** Restore 80 Aether daily.
- **Epione Center:** Treat Deep Trauma.
- **Research Facility:** Upgrade Protos Skills and Captain skills.
- **Excursions:** Use 3 daily Communication Passes to raise Affinity.
- **Nono's Shop:** Spend Policy Points on Research Notes.
