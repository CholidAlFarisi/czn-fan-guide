# Character & Account Progression Guide

Optimizing progression in *Chaos Zero Nightmare* requires allocating stamina (Aether) and resources across character levels, rank promotions, skill potentials, equipment, and Save Data loadouts.

---

## 1. Upgrade Priority Order

Follow this priority list when powering up your roster:

```
1. Level Up (Battle Memory)
   └── Fast base stat gains (ATK, DEF, HP)
2. Promotion (Striker / Class Manuals)
   └── Unlocks higher level caps (Lv 10, 20, 30, 40...)
3. Potential Upgrades
   └── Sequential node tree enhancing base cards and passives
4. Save Data Optimization
   └── Most impactful endgame performance multiplier
5. Partner Upgrades
   └── Stat links and auxiliary battle effects
6. Manifest Ego
   └── Gacha duplicate upgrades for enhanced ultimate skills
```

---

## 2. Progression Systems Explained

### Level Up (Battle Memory)
- Consumes **Battle Memory** items and credits to raise a character's current level.
- Provides immediate scaling to base HP, Attack, and Defense.

### Promotion (Class Manuals)
- When a combatant reaches a level cap (e.g., Lv 10, Lv 20), they must undergo **Promotion**.
- Requires class-specific manuals (e.g., **Striker Manuals**, Hunter Manuals, Vanguard Manuals).
- Unlocks the next tier of leveling and opens new branches in the Potential tree.

### Potential Upgrades
- A sequential talent grid unique to each character.
- Upgrading nodes permanently improves character base stats and enhances starting card values (e.g., higher damage percentages, lower AP costs, added tags).

### Save Data System
- **The single most impactful long-term upgrade in CZN.**
- Generated at the end of Chaos runs, Save Data acts as a permanent, high-power loadout equipped for non-roguelike content.
- Budgeted by Save Data Value — prioritize carrying over top-tier Epiphanies and unique equipment.

### Partner Upgrades
- Equipping and leveling Partners (such as members of Sileyma or academy companions) provides passive stat bonuses and supplementary in-combat assistance.

### Manifest Ego
- Enhanced by obtaining duplicate character **Memory Codes** from the gacha summoning banners.
- Each Manifest Ego stage upgrades the power, secondary effects, and utility of the combatant's ultimate Ego Skill.

## 3. Midgame Strategy (Captain Lv 25–40)

### Farm Memory Fragments (Lv 25+)
- Once **Simulation: Memory Fragments** unlocks at Captain Level 25, spend daily Aether here (60 Aether per run).
- Gear your core 3 combatants with solid stat distributions (ATK%, Crit Rate, Defense, Energy Regen).

### Prepare Two Distinct Teams (Basin of Hyperspace)
- Unlocked around Captain Level 30, the **Basin of Hyperspace** requires **two distinct, fully leveled teams**.
- Ensure you have built at least 6 combatants with complementary attributes and balanced card decks.

---

## 4. Endgame Transition (Captain Lv 40+)

### Farm Legendary Memory Fragments
- Reaching Level 40 unlocks higher difficulty simulation tiers where **Legendary (gold) Memory Fragments** drop.
- Maximize set bonuses and optimize substat rolls.

### Tackle the Zero System & Codices
- Unlock the **Zero System** at Captain Level 40.
- Clear conditional **Codices** (Difficulty 5+) to accumulate **Chaos Orbs**.
- Advance the **Chaos Matrix** for account-wide bonuses, loot certification cards, and divine encounters in the **Zero Archive**.
