# Chaos Zero Nightmare — Master Card Database

Complete catalogue of all 2,174 cards in Chaos Zero Nightmare, extracted directly from official game datasets.

## Database Overview
- **Total Cards Recorded**: 2174 (including Epiphany variations, Collapse skills, and Neutral cards)
- **Character-Specific Cards**: 1469
- **Neutral / Monster / Area Cards**: 705
- **Card Categories**: Attack (`CARD_ATK`), Skill (`CARD_SKILL`), Upgrade/Power (`CARD_POWER`), Collapse/Ailment (`CARD_COLLAPSE`)
