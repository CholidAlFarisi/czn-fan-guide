# Neutral Cards

Neutral cards are universal combat cards that are **not tied to any specific character**. They can be found during Chaos Manifestation runs through Shop nodes, event rewards, and investigation area encounters. The Neutral Card pool forms a critical component of flexible deckbuilding.

## 2. Neutral Card Categories

Neutral cards span all card types and tags:
- **Attack Cards:** General-purpose damage options without character restrictions.
- **Skill Cards:** Universal shields, heals, buffs, draws, and AP grants.
- **Upgrade Cards:** Passive stat boosts applicable to any character.
- **Special Tags:** Retain, Exhaust, Haste, Bullet variants, Lead, and Pulverize effects.

### Common Neutral Pool Themes
- **Universal Healing:** Heal 100% with or without Retain conditions.
- **Team Shields:** 100–200% Shield options applicable across attributes.
- **Card Draw:** Draw 1–3 cards with various conditions.
- **Burst Damage:** Standalone Attack cards scaling by attribute or condition.
- **Tag Enablers:** Cards granting Ephemeral, Bullet, Haste, or Unique tags for engine building.

---

## 4. How Neutral Cards Affect Run Strategy

- **Deck Consistency:** Adding neutral Attack or Skill cards can improve draw reliability in tight AP economies.
- **Tag Injection:** Neutral cards with Haste, Retain, or Bullet tags can enable off-character combos.
- **Exhaust Options:** Neutral Exhaust cards provide temporary power spikes without permanent deck bloat.
- **Shop Priorities:** During Chaos Mode shop nodes, neutral Upgrade cards and Retain enablers are often high-value purchases.
