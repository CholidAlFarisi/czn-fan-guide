# Ark City Social Hub & Management

**Ark City** is the central social headquarters, rest district, and operational management nexus in *Chaos Zero Nightmare*. Unlocked immediately upon clearing **Story Chapter 2 #01-2**, Ark City allows captains to oversee infrastructure, manage fleet morale, recover vital resources, upgrade fleet-wide operational capabilities, and deepen personal bonds with combatants.

---

## Facilities and Key Features

### 1. Policy Office
The bureaucratic and strategic command center of Ark City.
- **Dispatch Missions:** Assign idle combatants to specialized imperial administrative and defense operations.
- **Morale & Efficiency:** Increases character happiness and city management stats over time.
- **Resource Generation:** Produces steady passive income of Policy Points, Credits, and basic enhancement materials.

### 2. Starshine Diner
A vibrant communal restaurant popular among fleet officers and mercenaries.
- **Meal Ordering:** Order various dishes to grant temporary run buffs or stat enhancements.
- **Random Encounters:** Combatants appear dynamically at tables; speaking with them provides flavor lore and minor rapport boosts.

### 3. Garden Café
A serene botanical retreat designed to offer psychological sanctuary from the grim realities of space travel.
- **Aether Recovery:** Directly restores **80 Aether (Stamina)** once daily upon visiting.
- **Officer Interactions:** A quiet meeting spot where combatants frequently visit to decompress after intensive missions.

### 4. Epione Center
The advanced psychological and neural rehabilitation wing.
- **Deep Trauma Treatment:** When combatants endure catastrophic mental strain or accumulate persistent trauma during high-intensity Chaos expeditions, the Epione Center treats and cleanses **Deep Trauma** afflictions.
- **Mental Recovery:** Ensures units are mentally conditioned before entering high-tier Codices. Characters also appear here recovering from previous sorties.

### 5. Research Facility
The technical engineering division overseen by imperial scientists.
- **Protos Skill Upgrades:** Enhances your rewind capabilities during combat (improving the efficiency and versatility of the Sands of Time rewind system).
- **Captain Skills:** Unlocks permanent account-wide passive perks, increasing inventory space, bonus experience gain, and tactical combat adjustments.

### 6. Nono's Shop
The specialized black-market and requisition stall operated by Nono.
- **Currency:** Spends **Policy Points** accumulated via the Policy Office.
- **Key Goods:** Stocks critical **Research Notes**, rare talent tomes, and high-value progression blueprints.

## Social Systems: Affinity, Excursions & Gifts

Developing intimacy and camaraderie with your squad members is vital for unlocking combat bonuses, unique character voice lines, and personal profile dossiers.

### Communication Passes & Excursions
- Captains receive **3 Communication Passes** daily (resetting at the daily server reset).
- Passes are spent to invite specific combatants on **Excursions** across various Ark City recreational zones (e.g., Garden Café, shopping districts, observatories).
- Meaningful dialogue choices during excursions award substantial **Affinity Points**.

### Gift Giving
- Captains can gift tailored luxury items, personal trinkets, and faction-specific souvenirs to combatants.
- Matching a character's favorite gift type yields significant bonus Affinity multipliers, rapidly unlocking their personal **Affinity Tiers**, exclusive profile entries, and customized dialogue.
