# Zero System

The **Zero System** is the premier mid-to-late game endgame progression and challenge mode in *Chaos Zero Nightmare*. Unlocked when players attain **Captain Level 40**, it transforms the standard Chaos loop into a highly customizable, high-difficulty sector simulation system.

---

## 1. Codices & Conditional Chaos

The Zero System generates customized, conditional Chaos sectors known as **Codices**:
- **Difficulty Tiers:** Difficulty 5 and above.
- **Enemy Levels:** Enemies are scaled to Level 40+, possessing advanced affixes, elevated Tenacity bars, and relentless attack patterns.
- **Conditional Modifiers:** Each Codex applies specific environmental rules, such as AP penalties, attribute restrictions, accelerated enemy turn counts, or enforced card tags.

---

## 2. Currencies & The Chaos Matrix

Engaging with the Zero System revolves around specialized progression economies:

### Chaos Orbs
- A dedicated endgame currency earned by conquering Codex challenges.
- Spent in the Zero System tech tree to unlock permanent combat advantages inside Codices (e.g., bonus starting AP, extra rerolls, reduced Stress generation).

### Chaos Matrix
- An account-level reward milestone tracker.
- As the cumulative total of collected Chaos Orbs rises, the Chaos Matrix unlocks permanent account rewards, rare summon tickets, high-tier enhancement materials, and elite cosmetics.

---

## 3. High-Tier Rewards & Features

### Legendary Memory Fragments
- Codices are the premier farming ground for **Legendary Memory Fragments** (the highest tier of character gear), enabling optimal stat distributions and set bonuses.

### Loot Certification Cards
- Special certification items that can be staked or consumed during Codex runs to amplify reward payouts, guaranteeing bonus card drops and elite materials upon successful extraction.

### Seasonal Chaos Archive
- Allows players to access past seasonal event Chaos zones, providing an avenue to acquire legacy exclusive **Save Data loadouts** and seasonal equipment sets that are otherwise unobtainable.

---

## 4. Mythic Codices & The 4 Gods

At the pinnacle of the Zero System lie the elusive **Mythic Codices**:
- **Unique Anomaly Rate:** Possess a **40% chance** to generate as a **Unique Codex**.
- **Divine Encounters:** Within these unique sectors, captains have the rare opportunity to encounter one of the cosmic **4 Gods (Divine Representatives)**.
- **Lore & Progression:** Surviving encounters with these cosmic deities unlocks classified entries in the **Zero Archive**, sheds light on the cosmological origin of Chaos, and grants opportunities for **Divine Epiphanies**.
