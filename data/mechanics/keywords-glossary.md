# Keywords & Glossary

A comprehensive glossary of combat keywords, mechanical tags, status effects, and progression terms used in *Chaos Zero Nightmare*.

---

## Combat & Status Keywords

### Agony
- **Type:** Damage-over-Time (DoT) Debuff.
- **Effect:** Inflicts Fixed Damage at the end of the turn. Standard duration is 2 turns unless refreshed or modified by card effects (prominently used by Tressa and Lucas).

### Fortitude
- **Type:** Defensive Buff.
- **Effect:** Enhances defensive stability, damage resistance, and shield efficiency for the team.

### Wave
- **Type:** Character Resource / Mechanic (Mika).
- **Effect:** Stacks water resonance. Upon accumulating 3 stacks of Wave, triggers hydrodynamic resonance to generate additional Action Points (AP) or amplify healing output.

### Morale
- **Type:** Offensive Buff.
- **Effect:** Stacks to incrementally increase overall damage dealt by the squad's Attack cards.

### Vulnerable
- **Type:** Defensive Debuff.
- **Effect:** Target takes increased damage from all attack sources while this debuff is active.

### Commence the Hunt
- **Type:** Character Mechanic (Hugo).
- **Effect:** When using a Targeting Attack Card, inflicts an **Extra Attack**. Each stack increases Extra Attack damage by **+30%** (stacks up to a maximum of 5 times).

### Extra Attack
- **Type:** Combat Trigger.
- **Effect:** Automatic follow-up offensive strikes triggered by specific card conditions. Extra Attacks scale with the character's **Critical Chance** and **Bonus Damage** stats.

### Linked
- **Type:** Synergy Mechanic (Heidemarie).
- **Effect:** Cards marked or modified with Link status trigger chained cooperative effects and bonus burst damage when played in sequence.

### Quietus
- **Type:** Card Tag / Discard Mechanic.
- **Effect:** Interacts directly with discard synergy, activating bonus damage, card retrieval, or AP refunds when cards are discarded from hand.

### Ravage State
- **Type:** Combat State.
- **Effect:** State entered by an enemy when their **Tenacity Bar** is fully broken. Grants the player **+1 AP**, reduces squad Stress gain, and delays the enemy's Action Count.

### Impair
- **Type:** Debuff.
- **Effect:** Reduces enemy combat capabilities, action efficiency, or secondary proc rates.

### Weaken
- **Type:** Offensive Debuff.
- **Effect:** Decreases the outgoing damage dealt by the afflicted enemy.

### Mark
- **Type:** Targeting Debuff (Luke / Selena).
- **Effect:** Places a tracking beacon on target enemies, concentrating bullet impacts and amplifying critical strike vulnerability.

### Dark Cloud Stance / Dark Mist Blade Art
- **Type:** Character Kata / Stance Mechanics (Rin).
- **Effect:** Alternating sword forms. Entering Dark Cloud Stance modifies Rin's slashes, enabling Dark Mist Blade Art bonus damage counts and zero-cost combo transitions.

### Counterattack
- **Type:** Defensive/Offensive Proc (Maribell / Magna).
- **Effect:** Retaliates with defensive-based damage or retaliatory strikes when the team is attacked or when gaining shields.

### Silver Veil
- **Type:** Unique Defensive Buff (Khalipe).
- **Effect:** Khalipe's signature defensive aura, mitigating damage and reinforcing shield thresholds based on defense stats.

### Ember
- **Type:** Elemental Debuff (Mei Lin).
- **Effect:** Stacks lingering fire resonance on foes, detonating for massive Passion damage upon reaching threshold or when consumed by finisher attacks.

## Card Tags

- **Retain:** Card remains in your hand at the end of the turn instead of being sent to the Graveyard.
- **Exhaust:** Card is permanently removed from play for the remainder of the battle upon use.
- **Haste:** Playing this card does **not** decrease the enemy's Action Count.
- **Lead:** Has a chance to reduce its own AP cost by 1 at the start of each turn.
- **Outbreak of War:** Guaranteed to be drawn in your opening starting hand at battle onset.
- **Ephemeral:** Card exists temporarily and vanishes at turn end or under specified triggers.
- **Bullet:** Specialized ammunition cards used by ballistic combatants (Lucas, Luke) that exhaust upon firing.
- **Unique:** Only one active copy of this card can exist in your operational deck.
- **Pulverize:** Deals heavy-impact physical or crushing damage.
- **Celestial:** Cosmic-tier tag associated with divine and high-order skills.
- **Finale:** High-impact turn-closing or combat-finishing ability.
- **Creation:** Specialized conjured cards generated by Orlea (tiered ★, ★★, and ★★★).

## Currencies & Account Terms

- **Aether:** The primary stamina energy resource consumed to run story stages, simulations, and Chaos sectors.
- **Crystals:** Premium summoning and requisition currency used for combatant/partner gacha.
- **Affinity:** Relationship bond level between the Captain and individual combatants, cultivated through Ark City interactions and gifts.
- **Captain Level:** Global player account rank, gating feature unlocks, maximum character level caps, and the Zero System (unlocked at Lv 40).
- **Promotion:** Ascending a combatant's rank to break their level ceiling (e.g., Lv 10, Lv 20, etc.) using class-specific manuals.
- **Potential:** Sequential skill tree nodes that enhance base stats and upgrade starting card properties.
- **Memory Fragments:** High-grade equipment artifacts that provide primary stats, secondary substats, and set bonuses.
- **Policy Points:** Administrative currency generated by the Policy Office in Ark City, spent at Nono's Shop.
