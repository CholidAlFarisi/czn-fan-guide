# Relics & Equipment System in Chaos Zero Nightmare

Relics and Equipment are critical gear acquired during Chaos Manifestation runs, Simulation: Memory Fragments, and endgame Zero System Codices. They grant passive stat escalations, special card triggers, and unique combat passives.

## Relic Statistics
- **Total Relics in Database**: 557
- **Categories**: EQUIPMENT, EQUIPMENT_TRANSFORM
- **Equipment Types**: Weapon, Armor, Accessory
- **Rarities**: Common, Rare, Unique, Legendary

## Relic Reinforcement
- Relics can be enhanced using materials farmed from **Simulation: Memory Fragments** (unlocked at Captain Lv25).
- At Captain Lv40 (Difficulty 5+), **Legendary Memory Fragments** become drop targets.
- Extracted Relics attach to the character's **Save Data Loadout**, allowing their bonuses to be utilized in non-Chaos story and boss content.
