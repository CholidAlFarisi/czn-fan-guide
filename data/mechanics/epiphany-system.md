# Epiphany System

The **Epiphany System** is a dynamic, mid-run card evolution mechanic in *Chaos Zero Nightmare*. It introduces spontaneous, tactical transformations during combat, turning standard plays into game-changing maneuvers.

---

## 1. Visual Indicators & Activation

During battle, an Epiphany can trigger on drawn cards:
- **Visual Glint:** A distinct **yellow glint** sparkles around the affected card in hand.
- **Character Icon:** A glowing **light bulb icon** illuminates over the owning character on the HUD.
- **Turn Progress:** Each unit in your squad has an independent chance at the start of every turn to trigger an Epiphany on their drawn cards.

---

## 2. Core Mechanics

### 0-AP Play
- Playing an Epiphany-affected card **costs 0 Action Points (AP)**.
- **Enemy Turn Progression:** Even though it costs 0 AP, playing the card **still progresses the enemy's Action Count** (unless the card possesses the **Haste** tag).

### Card Transformation
- Basic starter cards gain potent bonus effects or can transform into completely new **character-exclusive Epiphany cards**.
- Epiphanies can permanently modify:
  - Card AP cost
  - Card tags (e.g., adding Retain, Haste, or Pulverize)
  - Damage/Shield scaling multipliers
  - Additional bonus effects (e.g., generating extra cards, inflicting debuffs, granting AP)

---

## 3. Epiphany Tiers

Epiphanies are graded across multiple progression tiers, scaling with run depth, character Potential, and difficulty level:

| Tier | Characteristics & Effects |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tier I** | Basic numerical enhancements (+damage, +shield percentage, minor debuff duration). |
| **Tier II** | Secondary conditional triggers (e.g., bonus hits if target is debuffed). |
| **Tier III** | Tag additions (e.g., grants Retain or reduces cost of subsequent cards). |
| **Tier IV** | Radical mechanical shifts (e.g., multi-target conversions, free extra plays). |
| **Tier V** | Apex character transformations, drastically altering card synergy loops. |
| **Divine Epiphany** | **The Highest Tier.** Cosmic-grade modifications granting overwhelming combat effects, often tied to encounters with the 4 Gods in the Zero Archive. |

---

## 4. Tactical Value

Epiphanies represent a **pivotal decision point** during Chaos runs:
- Deciding whether to play an Epiphany immediately for 0 AP or hold a Retained version for a burst combo turn.
- Balancing enemy Action Count thresholds against free card tempo.
- Guiding which Epiphanies are preserved and carried forward into the permanent **Save Data** power budget.
