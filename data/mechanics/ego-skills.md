# Ego Skills

An **Ego Skill** (or Manifest Ego) is a character's ultimate ability — the most powerful combat tool available per match. Each combatant possesses a singular Ego skill reflecting their unique tactical identity and thematic role.

---

## 1. Ego Points (EP) Economy

Before an Ego can be activated, the combatant must accumulate sufficient **Ego Points (EP)**, displayed as an energy gauge in the HUD.

**EP Gain Sources:**
- Using standard Action Points (AP) to play cards.
- Defeating enemy units.
- Completing a recovery sequence after recovering from Mental Breakdown.
- Ending your turn (passive accumulation).

**EP Cost:**
- Shown in the upper-left of the skill icon.
- Costs vary per character but generally scale with the magnitude of the ultimate effect (e.g., Kayron's Death Halo requires a high EP cost due to its devastating AoE damage potential).

---

## 2. Usage Limits

| Limitation | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Per-Combat Cap** | Typically usable **once per battle**. |
| **Breakthrough Exception** | A character who has undergone and survived a **Mental Breakdown** recovers and gains permission to cast their Ego Skill a second time in the same match. |

---

## 3. Manifest Ego Upgrade System

As you duplicate a combatant's **Memory Codes** through the gacha summoning system, you gain ability upgrade fragments:
- **Manifest Ego Upgrade:** Each tier unlocks enhanced passive abilities, secondary synergies, or scaled-up ultimate effects.
- Examples include increased raw damage output, added utility triggers, or expanded range effects.

---

## 4. Notable Ego Examples

- **Kayron — Death Halo:** Unleashes a devastating burst dealing **400% damage to all enemies**. Requires high EP investment but can single-handedly collapse challenging encounters.
- **Luke — Targeting Systems:** Deploys advanced Wolf Platoon targeting arrays for amplified marksmanship follow-ups.
- **Mika — Hydrodynamic Resonance:** Activates deep-tide wave manipulation, generating substantial AP at peak Wave stack thresholds.
- **Hugo — Commence the Hunt Apex:** Amplifies vanguard suppression tactics, granting massive Multi-hit extra attack sequences.
- **Rin — Dark Mist Blade Art Finale:** Unleashes her ultimate sword kata, consuming stance states to deliver catastrophic single-target Void damage.
- **Orlea — Sacred Creation Blessing:** Summons an apex divine entity granting team-wide shield and restoration.

*(Note: Exact EP costs and secondary effects for each character's Ego are dynamic based on version patches and Manifest Ego upgrade levels.)*
