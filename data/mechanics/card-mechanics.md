# Card Mechanics

Every card in *Chaos Zero Nightmare* contains a structured anatomy. Understanding the layers is essential for building synergistic decks and optimizing run performance.

---

## 1. Card Costs

Displayed in the upper-left corner of the card:
- **Numeric AP Cost (0–3):** The Action Points required to play the card. Higher cost generally indicates a more powerful effect.
- **0-Cost:** Free plays. Essential for combo extensions and zero-cost discard triggers.
- **X-Cost:** Consumes all remaining AP. Powerful finisher plays.
- **No Cost Display:** The card requires a specific condition or trigger instead of AP.

---

## 2. Card Types

Cards are categorized into four structural types:
- **Attack:** Deal direct damage. May include secondary effects such as buffing, debuffing, or creating cards.
- **Skill:** Utility effects. Healing, shields, buffs, debuffs, card draw, AP gain.
- **Upgrade:** Permanent stat boosts for the rest of combat. Typically removed from deck after play.
- **Status Ailment:** Debuff cards placed in your hand that occupy slots. Usually must be played or destroyed to prevent hand clogging.

---

## 3. Card Attributes

Each card has a color-coded attribute:
- **Passion** (Fire) — Associated with aggressive raw damage and AOE.
- **Void** (Violet) — Associated with discards, card creation, and cosmic effects.
- **Instinct** (Eye) — Associated with draws, shields, and support.
- **Order** (Cross) — Associated with bullets, criticals, and structured combos.
- **Justice** (Scale) — Associated with heals, shields, waves, and balance.

> **Rule:** Only *enemies* have attribute weaknesses. Hitting an enemy's weak attribute grants **+25% attack and break damage**. There is no attribute advantage or weakness between player characters.

---

## 4. Card Tags

Tags appear in the upper-right or as text overlays on the card:
- **Retain:** Stays in hand between turns.
- **Exhaust:** Removed from play permanently after use.
- **Ultimate:** Ends your turn immediately.
- **Lead:** Chance to reduce cost by 1 at turn start.
- **Outbreak of War:** Drawn first in the battle.
- **Haste:** Playing this card does not reduce the enemy Action Count.
- **Ephemeral:** Temporary effect under specific conditions.
- **Bullet:** Ammunition mechanics (Lucas, Luke).
- **Unique:** Only one copy per deck.
- **Linked:** Heidemarie's exclusive synergy tag.
- **Quietus:** Rewards card discarding.
- **Pulverize:** Heavy-impact tag.
- **Celestial:** High-tier cosmic effect.
- **Finale:** Game-ending ultimate play.

---

## 5. Card Effects

The text on the card defines its gameplay behavior. Effects are static at acquisition but can be modified by the **Epiphany System** during runs, unlocking stronger bonuses, cost reductions, or completely transformed card variants.

> **Card effects define builds and team compositions.** Always examine how an effect interacts with your deck's AP economy, card draw, and synergy loops.

---

## 6. Neutral Cards

- **Not character-specific** — universally applicable.
- Found in investigation areas, shops, and event nodes in Chaos Mode.
- The full neutral card pool contains **167+ cards** as of the 2026 Zero System update.
- See [`neutral-cards.md`](neutral-cards.md) for details.

---

## 7. Monster Cards

- Obtained from specific monsters in the **Chaos Manifestation** system.
- Often carry unique effects tied to the originating Chaos entity.
- Provide additional deck depth and can be highly powerful in save-data builds.
