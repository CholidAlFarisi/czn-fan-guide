# Chaos Mode

**Chaos Mode** (Chaos Manifestation) is the core roguelike gameplay loop of *Chaos Zero Nightmare*. Captains assemble an expeditionary squad of three combatants to navigate procedurally generated anomaly zones, draft cards, refine decks, and conquer cosmic threats.

---

## 1. Expedition Structure & Node Map

Upon selecting a target Chaos sector, players traverse a branching, node-based sector map:
- Each path offers distinct risk-versus-reward trade-offs.
- Progression culminates in an apex Sector Boss encounter.

### Node Zone Distribution
The standard expedition node distribution follows approximately:

| Zone Type | Distribution | Strategic Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Combat Zone** | ~70% | Standard, elite, and multi-wave battles against Chaos entities. Primary source of card rewards, battle memory, and Epiphany triggers. |
| **Event Zone** | ~20% | Narrative choices, psychological anomalies, gambling risks, or unique situational buffs/debuffs. |
| **Shop Zone** | ~5% | Spend in-run currency to purchase neutral cards, purge unwanted cards from the deck, and acquire equipment relics. |
| **Rest Zone** | ~5% | Safe zones to recover team HP, lower accumulated character Stress, or upgrade existing cards. |

---

## 2. In-Run Deckbuilding & Growth

During the expedition, your initial deck of 24 cards (8 per character) evolves dynamically:
- **Card Drafting:** Choose new Attack, Skill, or Upgrade cards after battle victories.
- **Card Pruning:** Remove basic or low-impact cards at Shops to increase deck consistency.
- **Epiphany Unlocks:** Discover mid-run card evolutions (Tiers I–V and Divine).
- **Relic / Equipment Acquisition:** Equip artifacts that provide passive combat bonuses or AP acceleration.

---

## 3. Difficulty Scaling & Modes

As your account progresses, increasingly difficult expedition tiers unlock:

### Standard Chaos Levels
- Higher Chaos levels introduce elite monster modifiers, increased enemy speed, and higher Tenacity shields.
- Commensurately scales drop rates for high-tier Memory Fragments and Save Data budget limits.

### Nightmare Difficulty
- **Unlock Requirement:** Cleared Story Chapter 3.
- **Modifiers:**
  - **2x Enemy HP**
  - **+50% Enemy Damage**
  - Significantly tightened turn timers and elevated enemy aggression.
- **Rewards:** Greatly enhanced drop rates for top-tier gear, high-grade enhancement manuals, and elevated Save Data ceilings.

### Deep Trauma Anomaly
- A high-stakes Chaos variant where psychological hazards are amplified.
- **Mechanics:** Stress rises exponentially faster, and mental breakdown afflictions are more punishing.
- **Payoff:** Substantially boosted reward yields, rare equipment drops, and peak-quality Save Data development.
