# Stress & Mental Breakdown

The **Stress System** is one of the definitive survival and psychological horror mechanics in *Chaos Zero Nightmare*. Managing your team's mental strain is just as vital as managing their physical health points.

---

## 1. The Stress Gauge

Each combatant has an individual **purple Stress Gauge** tracked on the character HUD:
- **Buildup Triggers:**
  - Taking direct unblocked HP damage from enemy attacks.
  - Dragged-out combat encounters (each additional combat round incrementally adds stress).
  - Certain high-tier enemy skills, debuffs, or psychological hazards.
- **Prevention:**
  - **Shields:** Absorbing attacks with shield points completely prevents stress accumulation from that hit.
  - **Healing:** Restores HP and mitigates overall party vulnerability.
  - **Ravage State:** Breaking an enemy's Tenacity bar significantly reduces incoming stress.

---

## 2. Mental Breakdown

When a combatant's Stress Gauge reaches **100**, they succumb to a **Mental Breakdown**:

### Immediate Consequences
1. **Blocked Team HP:** One-third (1/3) of the team's maximum HP is immediately locked/blocked.
2. **Immobilization:** The affected character becomes immobilized and cannot act normally.
3. **Card Corruption:** All cards belonging to that character in the deck, hand, and draw pile are converted into erratic **Breakdown Cards**.

### Recovery Mechanics
- To recover a character from Mental Breakdown, you must **play 5 Breakdown Cards**.
- Breakdown Cards often feature volatile costs, unpredictable outcomes, or tactical penalties.

### Post-Recovery State
- **Reduced EP Cost:** The recovered combatant gains a substantial discount on their **Ego Skill EP cost**.
- **HP Recovery:** The blocked one-third HP bar unlocks, but your team only recovers **half (50%) of the lost HP**.
- **Ego Skill Reset:** A character who recovers from a breakdown is granted an additional opportunity to cast their powerful **Ego Skill** during the match.

---

## 3. Deep Trauma

**Deep Trauma** is a severe, high-stakes variation encountered in deep Chaos Manifestations and endgame Codices:
- **Accelerated Stress:** The Stress Gauge fills significantly faster from all sources.
- **Amplified Mental Afflictions:** Characters afflicted with Deep Trauma suffer permanent or persistent negative psychological debuffs during runs.
- **High-Risk, High-Reward:** Runs featuring Deep Trauma offer vastly superior loot, higher drop rates of rare Memory Fragments, and accelerated Save Data growth potential.
- **Treatment:** Deep Trauma afflictions persist after the run until treated at the **Epione Center** in Ark City.
