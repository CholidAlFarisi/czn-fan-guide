Combat System

Turn-based card combat: draw 5 cards, spend 3 AP, chain synergies, and exploit attribute weaknesses for +25% damage.

🧠

Mental Breakdown

Stress builds from unblocked damage. At 100, a Combatant breaks down — team HP is blocked by a third until recovery.

💾

Save Data

After each Chaos run, build a permanent loadout. Save Value limits what carries over — higher difficulty means better saves.

💡

Epiphany

Cards evolve mid-run. Epiphany plays cost 0 AP and can transform basics into character-exclusive power cards.

🌀

Chaos Mode

Procedural roguelike runs through combat, event, shop, and rest zones. Permadeath feeds persistent progression.

⚙️

Zero System

Endgame Chaos with Codex modifiers and Chaos Orbs. Higher risk, far stronger Save Data and exclusive rewards.

Universe

Lore & Factions

Eons ago, an unknown alien life form devastated Earth — and it is still spreading across the universe. This corruption is known as Chaos.

Humanity survives among the stars as a space-faring empire. You are a genetically engineered imperial officer leading an expeditionary force to eradicate the Chaos infestation — until a routine mission strips you of your memory, an event that should be impossible, and pulls you into a conspiracy involving warring factions of the empire and the true nature of Chaos itself.

A dark blend of science fiction and psychological horror, where every dive into Chaos leaves marks on the mind — and every recovery shapes the story.
