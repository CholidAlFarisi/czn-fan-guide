Gameplay Engine

Mechanics, Deities & Systems

Comprehensive breakdown of card combat, mental breakdowns, the 6 Primordial Deities, 491 Divine Sparks, 557 Relics, and the complete 28-term combat glossary.

⚔️

Turn-Based Card Combat

Every battle turn starts with your 3-hero squad drawing 5 cards and receiving 3 Action Points (AP). Cards share a unified team HP pool and can be cast in any sequence to weave devastating chain reactions.

🧠

Mental Breakdown & Ego Recovery

Unblocked damage builds psychological Stress. At 100 Stress, a hero suffers a Mental Breakdown: team max HP drops by 1/3 and normal cards morph into Breakdown Cards. Playing 5 Breakdown Cards purges the trauma and unlocks their 0 AP Ego Skill.

💥

Tenacity & Ravage State

Depleting an enemy's purple Tenacity gauge forces them into a Ravage State. This stuns their Action Counter, mitigates team Stress gain, and immediately awards your squad +1 bonus AP for relentless punishment.

💡

Epiphany Mutation System

Mid-run cards mutate into powerful specialized Epiphany variants (Branches I–V). Epiphanies grant 0 AP cost casts, draw acceleration, and persistent combat tags, dramatically altering character playstyles.

🌀

Chaos Mode Roguelike Progression

Explore branching sector paths featuring Combat, Elites, Shrines, Merchants, and Rest stops. Manage Stress and health across consecutive sectors—permadeath claims the run but unlocks permanent account research notes.

💾

Save Data & Zero System

Completed runs crystallize into permanent Save Data loadouts. Higher Codex heat modifiers expand your Save Value budget, allowing you to lock top-tier mutated cards and relics for boss battles and future conquests.

Epiphany Mutation & Evolution Architecture

304 UNIQUE BRANCHES • 92 COMMON SPARKS • 6 DEITIES

Epiphany is the pinnacle card mutation mechanic in Chaos Zero Nightmare. In the Roguelike Chaos Mode, combatant cards evolve across 5 specialized branches, gaining dramatic multiplier amplifications, AP cost reductions (-1 AP), shield conversions, multi-hit counts, and unique synergy activations.

⚠️
Aturan Fundamental: Basic Card Tidak Memiliki Epiphany

Basic card (kartu starter standar combatant seperti Strike dan Defend) tidak dapat berevolusi ke cabang Epiphany maupun dipasangi Common/Divine Sparks. Sistem Epiphany hanya berlaku eksklusif untuk Unique Combatant Cards (Branch 1–5) dan kartu save data non-starter.

✦ BRANCH 1
Pure Potency & Scaling

Massive direct multiplier increase (e.g. 260% → 450% DEF DMG). Pure output for primary burst cards.

✦ BRANCH 2
Hit-Count & Debuffs

Multiplies hit counts or applies Mark, Vulnerable, and Agony to prime bosses for execution.

✦ BRANCH 3
Kit Synergy & Stances

Interlocks with core passive stances (e.g. generating Effulgent Blade or charging Overdrive meters).

✦ BRANCH 4
Tactical AoE Conversion

Transforms single-target strikes into squad-wide AoE destruction or converts DEF to True Damage.

✦ BRANCH 5
Retention & Loop Engine

Shield Retention (preserves barrier between rounds), Hand cycling, and AP refund loops.

The 6 Primordial Cosmic Deities & 491 Divine Sparks

During Chaos expeditions, Captains encounter cosmological shrines governed by six cosmic deities. Shrines bestow Divine Sparks—permanent passive card alterations that modify AP costs, damage multipliers, and cooperative pincer triggers.

CALIGO Tenacity & Ravage

Governs break damage and berserker scaling. Sparks provide +100% Ravage scaling, critical damage multipliers, and convert lost squad HP into raw offensive power.

Signature Spark: Increases Ravage state duration and grants true damage on break.
CIRCEN Action Points & Time

Master of temporal acceleration and card economy. Sparks grant -1 AP cost reductions on signature skills, energy rebates upon 3+ card chains, and AP refunds.

Signature Spark: Every 4th card played in a turn refunds 1 AP.
DIALLOS Shield & Aegis

Deity of impenetrable bulwarks. Sparks amplify Vanguard shields by +30%, grant Metallization stacks that absorb Stress, and prevent squad lethal damage once per battle.

Signature Spark: Convert 50% of remaining shield at turn end into permanent armor.
NIHILUM Void & Hand Flow

Cosmic void manipulating card cycle. Sparks expand starting hand draw by +1, retain key cards across turns, and trigger bonus AoE burst when deck reshuffles.

Signature Spark: Draw 2 extra cards whenever a card is discarded from hand.
SECRED Pincer & Synergy

Deity of unison and cooperative tactics. Sparks guarantee 100% Pincer joint attacks when companion conditions are met, triggering auxiliary supporter strikes.

Signature Spark: Supporter partner strikes trigger on every Attack card played.
VITOR Destruction & Bleed

Lord of absolute slaughter. Multiplies final damage output by +30%, inflicts true damage armor penetration, and triggers catastrophic Bleed explosions.

Signature Spark: Increase card damage by +30% with zero additional AP cost.
Client Engine Formula

Official Combat Damage Formula

Extracted directly from the official client battle engine (procDamageCalculate and stat_formula). All attacks, modifiers, barrier interactions, and elemental multipliers resolve in strict sequential layers:

Final Damage = [(Base ATK × Skill Multiplier) + Flat Additions] × Ego Affinity Mult × Crit Mult × [1000 / (1000 + DEF)] × (1 + Final Dmg Bonus)
1. Base ATK & Stat Transfer
Combatant baseline ATK + Partner Stat Transfer (11%–15% ATK) + Weapon flat ATK + in-combat flat buffs.
2. Ego Affinity Multiplier
Weakness Advantage deals +30% damage (1.30×). Resistance reduces damage by -25% (0.75×). Secred Divine Epiphanies make attacks Always Weakness.
3. Critical Strike Factor
Base Crit Damage is 150% (1.50×). Modified additively by Memory Fragments, Vitor Sparks, and Striker passives up to 300%+.
4. Defense & Shield Absorption
Damage first strikes Shield (Barrier). Shields prevent team Stress gain. Damage penetrating shields is reduced by 1000 / (1000 + Target DEF).
5. Tenacity Break (Stagger)
Depleting enemy Tenacity to 0 triggers Break State: delays enemy action for 1 turn and applies a flat +50% Break Damage Multiplier.
6. Coordinated Pincer Strike
Certain cards trigger an off-turn assist from adjacent allies, delivering a bonus strike at 50%–100% of their base attack power without spending AP.

Tactical Supporter Rules

Partner Compatibility, Restrictions & Ego Resonance

Partners (Supporters) are auxiliary operatives paired 1-to-1 with your frontline Combatants. In CZN v1.0.84, partner equipping is governed by three strict tactical rules:

🔒

1-to-1 Unique Equipping

Each Combatant has exactly 1 Partner slot (total 3 Partners per squad). A Partner cannot be duplicated across multiple squad members. Once assigned to Slot 01, that partner is unavailable for Slot 02 or 03.

📊

Base Stat Transfer

Partners transfer 12%–16% HP, 11%–15% ATK, and 14%–18% DEF directly to the equipped hero. High-rarity 5★ partners provide higher stat yields and +35 Flat ATK bonuses.

🔥

Ego Resonance & Soulmate Bonus

When equipped to a hero sharing the partner's Ego Element or canonical identity (belong_character_eff), they activate Ego Resonance: grants +15% bonus stat transfer and unlocks their exclusive 0 AP Ego Passive skill!
