Gameplay Engine

Mechanics, Deities & Systems

Comprehensive breakdown of card combat, mental breakdowns, the 6 Primordial Deities, 491 Divine Sparks, 557 Relics, and the complete 28-term combat glossary.

⚔️

Turn-Based Card Combat

Every battle turn starts with your 3-hero squad drawing 5 cards and receiving 3 Action Points (AP). Cards share a unified team HP pool and can be cast in any sequence to weave devastating chain reactions.

🧠

Mental Breakdown & Ego Recovery

Unblocked damage builds psychological Stress. At 100 Stress, a hero suffers a Mental Breakdown: team max HP drops by 1/3 and normal cards morph into Breakdown Cards. Playing 5 Breakdown Cards purges the trauma and unlocks their 0 AP Ego Skill.

💥

Tenacity & Ravage State

Depleting an enemy's purple Tenacity gauge forces them into a Ravage State. This stuns their Action Counter, mitigates team Stress gain, and immediately awards your squad +1 bonus AP for relentless punishment.

💡

Epiphany Mutation System

Mid-run cards mutate into powerful specialized Epiphany variants (Branches I–V). Epiphanies grant 0 AP cost casts, draw acceleration, and persistent combat tags, dramatically altering character playstyles.

🌀

Chaos Mode Roguelike Progression

Explore branching sector paths featuring Combat, Elites, Shrines, Merchants, and Rest stops. Manage Stress and health across consecutive sectors—permadeath claims the run but unlocks permanent account research notes.

💾

Save Data & Zero System

Completed runs crystallize into permanent Save Data loadouts. Higher Codex heat modifiers expand your Save Value budget, allowing you to lock top-tier mutated cards and relics for boss battles and future conquests.

The 6 Primordial Cosmic Deities & 491 Divine Sparks

During Chaos expeditions, Captains encounter cosmological shrines governed by six cosmic deities. Shrines bestow Divine Sparks—permanent passive card alterations that modify AP costs, damage multipliers, and cooperative pincer triggers.

CALIGO Tenacity & Ravage

Governs break damage and berserker scaling. Sparks provide +100% Ravage scaling, critical damage multipliers, and convert lost squad HP into raw offensive power.

Signature Spark: Increases Ravage state duration and grants true damage on break.
CIRCEN Action Points & Time

Master of temporal acceleration and card economy. Sparks grant -1 AP cost reductions on signature skills, energy rebates upon 3+ card chains, and AP refunds.

Signature Spark: Every 4th card played in a turn refunds 1 AP.
DIALLOS Shield & Aegis

Deity of impenetrable bulwarks. Sparks amplify Vanguard shields by +30%, grant Metallization stacks that absorb Stress, and prevent squad lethal damage once per battle.

Signature Spark: Convert 50% of remaining shield at turn end into permanent armor.
NIHILUM Void & Hand Flow

Cosmic void manipulating card cycle. Sparks expand starting hand draw by +1, retain key cards across turns, and trigger bonus AoE burst when deck reshuffles.

Signature Spark: Draw 2 extra cards whenever a card is discarded from hand.
SECRED Pincer & Synergy

Deity of unison and cooperative tactics. Sparks guarantee 100% Pincer joint attacks when companion conditions are met, triggering auxiliary supporter strikes.

Signature Spark: Supporter partner strikes trigger on every Attack card played.
VITOR Destruction & Bleed

Lord of absolute slaughter. Multiplies final damage output by +30%, inflicts true damage armor penetration, and triggers catastrophic Bleed explosions.

Signature Spark: Increase card damage by +30% with zero additional AP cost.

557 Relics & Equipment Tiers

Relics in *Chaos Zero Nightmare* are permanent gear artifacts discovered during Chaos explorations, elite boss drops, and simulation runs. Characters equip weapons, armor, and accessories that grant passive stat scaling and combat triggers.

Unique Tiers
Game-defining mutation relics with archetype triggers (e.g. Twin Swords, Shell Bug Horn).
Legend Tiers
Endgame artifacts dropping in high-heat Zero System runs with massive dual stats.
Rare Tiers
Core mid-game equipment forming baseline set bonuses and stability.
Common Tiers
Early progression gear acquired from story stages and standard simulation runs.
